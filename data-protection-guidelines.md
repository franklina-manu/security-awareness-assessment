# Safe Handling of Company and Customer Information

## Purpose
Promote careful handling of information that could harm people or the organisation if lost, exposed or altered.

## Practical safeguards
- Access only the information needed for your role.
- Share sensitive files only with authorised recipients and through approved channels.
- Check recipient addresses and permissions before sending information.
- Use organisation-approved storage and collaboration tools.
- Avoid downloading sensitive data to personal devices or unapproved cloud services.
- Lock your screen when away from your workstation.
- Keep paper records secure and dispose of them using approved confidential-waste processes.
- Do not discuss confidential matters in public places.
- Follow retention and deletion rules; do not keep information longer than permitted.
- Report lost devices, misdirected emails or suspected data exposure immediately.

## Before sharing a file, ask:
1. Is the recipient authorised to receive it?
2. Is this the minimum information they need?
3. Is the sharing method approved and suitably protected?
4. Have I checked the recipient and access permissions?
5. Is there a business reason to share it?

## If information is sent to the wrong person
- Report it immediately through the organisation's incident-reporting process.
- Provide the details requested by the response team.
- Do not attempt to conceal or independently investigate the incident.
- Follow instructions about recalling messages, revoking access or notifying affected parties.

## Key message
Protect information throughout its lifecycle: create, access, use, share, store, retain and dispose of it securely.
