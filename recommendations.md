# Security Awareness Recommendations and Follow-up Plan

## Purpose
These recommendations show how SecureCorp could strengthen employee awareness. They are illustrative and should be adapted to the organisation's actual risks, policies and regulatory obligations.

## Recommended actions
| Area | Recommended action | Suggested frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Phishing | Provide examples of suspicious messages and explain how to report them | At onboarding and periodic refreshers |
| Passwords and MFA | Explain unique passwords, approved password managers and MFA prompt safety | At onboarding and when policy changes |
| Social engineering | Practise verification of unusual requests and identity claims | Periodic team discussions |
| Data protection | Reinforce approved storage, sharing and disposal procedures | At onboarding and periodic refreshers |
| Incident reporting | Make reporting channels easy to find and explain what details to provide | Regular reminders |

## Suggested measurement
An organisation could monitor:
- quiz results by topic, using aggregated results where appropriate;
- attendance or completion of approved training;
- the number and quality of employee reports of suspicious messages;
- recurring questions or knowledge gaps; and
- time taken to escalate reported incidents.

Metrics should be interpreted carefully. An increase in reports may indicate improved awareness rather than an increase in attacks.

## Example improvement cycle
1. Deliver awareness material.
2. Run the knowledge check.
3. Identify topics where participants need more support.
4. Provide targeted refresher guidance.
5. Repeat the assessment after an appropriate interval.
6. Review feedback and update the material.

## Limitations
This document is a sample awareness plan, not a formal risk assessment, audit, legal opinion or proof of regulatory compliance. Any real programme should be approved by the organisation and aligned with its policies, applicable law and reporting procedures.
